# Country flags

The pictures in this folder (`<two-letter country code>.png`, 48 x 36 px) are the 4:3 flags of the open-source
**flag-icons** project (https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licence, copyright Panayiotis Lipiridis.
They were turned into small PNG files with `rsvg-convert -w 48 -h 36`. Used by the Top 100 view (a flag under a firm's rank).
